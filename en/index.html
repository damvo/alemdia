Before you sign

What to ask a management company

  • The commission: what percentage, and on what amount (with or without cleaning, before or after platform fees).
  • What is charged on top: cleaning, laundry, consumables, photos, onboarding fee.
  • How long the contract lasts and the notice period to leave.
  • Who keeps the listing and the reviews if you change company.
  • Who is recorded as operator of the rental in the RNAL.
  • When you receive your share, and with what statement.
  • How you block dates for your own use.

Frequently asked questions

Holiday rental management in Portugal: FAQ

What does a holiday rental management company do?

It runs the day-to-day operation of the Alojamento Local on the owner's behalf: listings and prices, guest messages, check-in, cleaning, maintenance and Portuguese legal obligations. Included services vary from one contract to another.

How much does a management company cost?

Usually a commission on the revenue of each booking. The percentage and what it includes vary from one company to another. Some also charge an onboarding fee or cleaning separately.

Can I use my property whenever I want?

It depends on the contract. Some companies let you block dates in the calendar for your own use. Check before signing.

Commission or fixed rent?

There are two models. With a commission, the company takes a share of each booking. With a fixed or guaranteed rent, the owner receives an agreed amount that does not depend on bookings. Some companies offer both: compare them over the same period.

Can I end the contract with the company?

It depends on the contract. Look at the minimum term, the notice period, any penalties, and what happens to confirmed bookings and to the listing. Check before signing.

Who pays for damage caused by guests?

It depends on the contract and the insurance. Ask the company how it handles damage (deposit, platform guarantee, insurance) and who pays the part none of them covers.

I live abroad. Which Portuguese obligations apply to my rental?

Registration with the municipality and the registration number in every listing, the identification plaque at the entrance, third-party liability insurance with a minimum cover of 75 000 € per claim, the report of foreign guests through SIBA within three working days, the physical and electronic complaints book and, up to 10 guests, a fire extinguisher, a fire blanket, a first aid kit and the 112 number on display (Decree-Law 128/2014 as amended by Decree-Law 76/2024, Turismo de Portugal guide).

Is AL em Dia a management company?

No. AL em Dia checks registrations in the RNAL and, at the owner's request, passes the contact to a management company in the area. The terms and the contract are agreed directly with the company. AL em Dia is paid by the management company when the management contract is signed and charges the owner nothing.
