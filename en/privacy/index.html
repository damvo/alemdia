Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026.

Who processes your data

AL em Dia (alemdia.pt), Lisbon, Portugal. Contact: privacidade@alemdia.pt.

What data we collect

Only what you type in the form: your mobile number, your email if you give it, the registration number of your Alojamento Local if you checked it, and the options you ticked. We also keep the date and time of sending, the page you sent it from, the site you came from, the code of the link we sent you if you came through it, the language of the page and the time spent on the page, as proof of your consent and to know how you found us.

The registration check runs in your browser, with the public data of the Registo Nacional de Alojamento Local. If you do not accept visit analytics, the number you type in the check box is not sent to us unless you submit the form. If you accept, it is recorded (see “Visit analytics”).

What for

Only for the purposes you tick:

Contact by a management company. We pass your mobile number, your email if you gave it, and the registration number to a holiday rental management company working in your area, so that it contacts you by phone or WhatsApp and presents its terms. Legal basis: your consent.

Follow-up of the request. About 30 days after you request a contact from a management company, we send you a single message, by email or WhatsApp, asking whether the company contacted you and whether you signed a contract. Answering is optional. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in knowing whether the service was provided.

Alerts about your registration. We send you messages by WhatsApp or email when there are relevant changes to your rental's registration or in your area. Legal basis: your consent.

Who receives it

The management company we introduce you to, only if you ticked that option. Google, whose Google Sheets service we use to store contacts and analytics data, and which processes the data on our behalf under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

How long

Until you withdraw your consent or until the data is no longer useful for the stated purposes.

Your rights

You can withdraw your consent at any time by replying STOP to a message, clicking the link at the end of each email or writing to privacidade@alemdia.pt. You can also ask for access, rectification, erasure or portability of your data, or object to its processing. If you consider your rights have not been respected, you can lodge a complaint with the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (cnpd.pt).

Visit analytics

Only if you click “Accept” on the analytics notice, we record: the pages you view, clicks, how far you scroll, time spent, the form fields you touch, the registration numbers you check, the language, the time zone, the screen size and the browser type.

To recognise your browser on a later visit, we store a random identifier in it (browser local storage, equivalent to a cookie). If you came through a link we sent you, that link contains a code that ties the visit to the message we sent you, and therefore to you. A checked registration number makes it possible to identify the holder of the rental in the public RNAL data.

Legal basis: your consent. You can change your choice at any time under “Analytics”, at the bottom of the municipality pages. We keep this data for as long as it is useful to understand how the site is used.

If you received a message from us

We contact Alojamento Local holders using public data: the Registo Nacional de Alojamento Local (dados.gov.pt) and the contact details that companies and holders publish online. We use the name or company name, the tax number, the registration numbers and the business contact details, only to introduce AL em Dia. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in making the service known. You can object at any time by replying STOP or writing to privacidade@alemdia.pt. We keep this data until you object or until it is no longer useful for this purpose.

Source of the public data

Registo Nacional de Alojamento Local, Turismo de Portugal, published on dados.gov.pt (CC BY 4.0 licence). This site is not a Turismo de Portugal service.
