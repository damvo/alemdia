Frequently asked questions

Holiday rentals in Lisbon: FAQ

How many holiday rentals are registered in Lisbon?

On 7 November 2026, the national Alojamento Local register (RNAL) listed 11 857 active registrations in the municipality of Lisbon: 10 533 apartments, 986 guest houses, 145 houses, 114 hostels and 79 rooms. 82 new rentals were registered in the last 180 days.

How many Alojamento Local registrations were cancelled in Lisbon?

In early February 2026, Lisbon city council ordered the cancellation of 6 765 Alojamento Local registrations for lack of proof of insurance (ECO, 20/02/2026).

Can I register a new holiday rental in Lisbon?

Lisbon's municipal Alojamento Local regulation, amended in December 2025, sets areas of absolute containment, where new registrations are in principle forbidden, and of relative containment, where they need the council's express authorisation. Santa Maria Maior, Misericórdia and Santo António are in absolute containment, São Vicente, Arroios and Estrela in relative containment. Check the address with Lisbon city council.

I live abroad. Can a management company run my rental in Lisbon?

Yes. A management company runs the day-to-day operation on the owner's behalf: listings and prices, guest messages at any hour, check-in, cleaning, repairs and Portuguese legal deadlines. It sends a statement and transfers your share. Included services vary from one contract to another.

What are the legal obligations of an Alojamento Local in Portugal?

Registration with the municipality and the registration number in every listing, the identification plaque at the entrance, third-party liability insurance with a minimum cover of 75 000 € per claim, the report of foreign guests through SIBA within three working days, the physical and electronic complaints book and, up to 10 guests, a fire extinguisher, a fire blanket, a first aid kit and the 112 number on display (Decree-Law 128/2014 as amended by Decree-Law 76/2024, Turismo de Portugal guide).

What happens if I do not submit proof of insurance?

The municipality can ask for proof of the third-party liability insurance at any time. Without an answer within the deadline, the mayor can cancel the registration, after a prior hearing, and the rental must stop at once (articles 9 and 13-A of Decree-Law 128/2014).

How much does it cost to request a contact?

Nothing. Leave your mobile number, tick what you agree to, and a management company working in Lisbon contacts you with its terms. If you go ahead, the company charges a commission on revenue, stated in its terms. AL em Dia is paid by the management company, not by the owner.
